Apr 2025 3rd Place 🥉

HELMo Industry 5.0 Hackathon

Liege, Belgium · HELMo & Technifutur

Built a web application utilizing open data from multiple Belgian databases, including regional solar panel electricity production data. Users could input their region, list their household devices, and optionally specify per-device power consumption (we had average values as fallback for most common appliances). The app calculates exactly how many solar panels they need and how many would actually be worth the investment.

The theme was Industry 5.0 - putting humans at the center of technological processes. Presented the solution to the jury and placed 3rd overall.

Born and raised in Poland. Spent six years in music school alongside regular education, studying music theory, history, and playing piano from age 7 to 12. That training shaped how I think: pattern recognition, discipline, and a structured approach to complex problems.

At 18 I moved to Belgium alone to study cybersecurity at Howest in Brugge, drawn by the program's reputation and the challenge of building an independent life in a new country. During Spring 2026 I interned at Advantech in Tokyo, building autonomous drones with AI-powered navigation.
